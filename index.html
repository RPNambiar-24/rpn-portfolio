Research Hijack 0.51 → 0.40

ATLAS-RAG

Trust-Aware Secure RAG · Final-Year Research

A reranker that fuses query trust (intent drift, injected instructions) with document trust (relevance, consistency, contradiction) to defend multi-hop agentic RAG against poisoning. Across a reproducible benchmark of 5 methods, 3 attack classes and 2 datasets, it cut query-hijack rate vs. vanilla RAG from 0.51 to 0.40 (0.36 to 0.28 under stealth attacks) with the best EM/F1. Fixing silent CPU inference gave a 4.5x speedup.
